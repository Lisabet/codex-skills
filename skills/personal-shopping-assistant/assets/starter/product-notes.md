# Product notes

Evaluate ingredients, taste, convenience, preparation method, and the decision to buy again independently.

## Successful

For each entry, record the exact product and store, what worked, the date, evidence, and whether `buy_again` was explicitly confirmed.

## Unsuccessful or do not repeat

State the scope: exact package, brand, category, store, or season. Do not broaden it silently.

## Uncertain

Put products here when only a public rating, purchase, ingredient list, or another partial signal is known.
